Description of HTTP Custom - AIO Tunnel VPN

HTTP Custom is an all-in-one tunnel VPN client for Android. You bring your own server or configuration, and the app handles the rest: building the tunnel, routing your device traffic through it, and giving you full control over how the connection is shaped.

It is a client, not a server. We do not sell or provide VPN servers, and we never see the traffic you send through your own.

SUPPORTED PROTOCOLS
• SSH — direct, over SSL/TLS, or with a custom HTTP payload
• V2Ray — VMess, VLESS, Trojan, Shadowsocks, SOCKS5, HTTP proxy, WireGuard and Hysteria2, with RAW, WebSocket, gRPC, HTTP Upgrade, XHTTP and mKCP transports, plus TLS and REALITY
• OpenVPN — import your own .ovpn profile
• UDP Custom — high-throughput UDP tunnelling
• Psiphon and SlowDNS for restricted networks

BUILT FOR CONTROL
• Custom HTTP request payloads with reusable tokens
• SNI and DNS changer, including IPv6 DNS and fallback DNS
• Per-app VPN — choose exactly which apps use the tunnel
• Adjustable MTU, IPv6 routing and auto-reconnect
• Multi-protocol profiles you can save, sort and switch between
• Load balancing across several accounts or servers
• Routing rules with one-tap presets
• Fragment and noise (anti-DPI) for TLS and REALITY handshakes
• Save and reopen your whole setup as a single config file

TOOLS INCLUDED
• One Cloud — open a configuration shared by a provider
• IP Finder and Auto Ping to find a working endpoint
• Share Net — pass your connection to other devices over a hotspot
• Response checker and a live connection log

PRIVACY
Connection details are normally stored encrypted on your device. They leave the device only when required by an action you request. The optional Short URL feature shows a separate disclosure before sending data to us. There is no account system, no registration, and no general usage telemetry of our own. The app shows ads through Google AdMob; what that SDK collects is described in our privacy policy.

REQUIREMENTS
No root required. Android 7.0 or newer. A working server or configuration of your own is required — the app does not come with one.
